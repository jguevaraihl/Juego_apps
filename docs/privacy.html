1. Summary

El Kiosko is a game that works entirely offline. We do not collect personal data, we do not send it to any server, and we do not share it with third parties. You do not need an account or to provide any information to play.

2. What is stored

The app stores your game progress on your phone only, in the app's private storage:

  • the state of your counter and your products;
  • your coins, experience and shop level;
  • active orders, the day's missions and the products you have discovered;
  • your sound, vibration, animation, language and appearance preferences;
  • the date and time of the last save, to work out what your shop sold while you were away.

None of this leaves your device.

3. What is NOT collected

We do not access your location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, calendar, phone number or messages. We do not read advertising identifiers or device identifiers. We do not know who you are.

4. Permissions

The app requests a single permission, and only if you turn it on: showing notifications (Android 13 and above). It is used for three reminders — that your till is full, that your helper's shift is over, or that you have saved enough coins to upgrade — all scheduled on your own phone. It is off by default; you can turn it on or off at any time from Settings or from Android's own settings. If you decline, the app works exactly the same.

Those reminders do not go through any server. There is no push, no token and no account: it is your phone's clock reminding you.

The app also uses the vibration permission, which Android grants automatically and does not require your approval. You can turn vibration off in Settings.

We request no other permissions.

5. Third parties

This version contains no advertising, no analytics tools, and no third-party services that receive information about you. If that changes, this policy will be updated before that version is published (see section 10).

6. If you write to us

From Settings → Your feedback you can send us a comment. That opens your phone's mail app: you send the message yourself, from your own account, and you can read and edit all of it before sending.

So that a comment is actually useful, the message comes with a few technical details already written at the end — the app version, your shop level, your player level and your language. They are in plain sight and you can delete them before sending. They contain nothing that identifies you.

If you write to us, we receive your email address and whatever you tell us, because that is how email works. We use that only to reply to you and to fix the game, and we do not share it with anyone.

7. How to delete your data

Since all the information is on your phone, you can delete it completely:

  • by uninstalling the app, or
  • from Android Settings → Apps → El Kiosko → Storage → Clear data.

We keep no copy, because we never receive that information.

If you have Android backup enabled, your own phone may back up the game's data to your Google account. That backup is controlled by you from Android's settings and lives under your account, not ours.

8. Children

This app is intended for adults. It is neither designed nor marketed for children, and we do not collect information from anyone.

9. Your rights

Various laws — including the EU GDPR, the UK GDPR, California's CCPA, Brazil's LGPD and Chile's Law 21.719 — grant rights of access, rectification, erasure, objection and portability over personal data.

Because this app collects no personal data, transmits none, and stores none on any server of ours or of a third party, there is no database against which to exercise those rights: all your information is on your phone and under your control. Even so, you can write to us at jguevaraihl+privacidad@gmail.com with any question.

10. Changes to this policy

If we add advertising, in-app purchases or analytics tools in the future, we will update this policy before publishing that version, and we will set out what information is collected, for what purpose and with whom it is shared.

The change history of this page is public and can be reviewed in the project's repository.
